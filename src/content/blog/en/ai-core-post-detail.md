Articles use the visual language of an AI control room: a network background, OUTPUT panel and Red Queen monitor surround the prose. They express an interest in AI and cinema, not model calls or an assessment of credibility.

Prose appears immediately and its panel stays still. Contents, code copying and image previews assist reading; the network, light bands and interactive monitor preserve the scene’s identity.

OUTPUT shows word count and estimated tokens, derived from prose unless manually overridden. They are not actual model usage. Reading time, latency and confidence are not displayed. Example model, mode and state labels are author-supplied interface metadata.
