Decoder Lab honors cypherpunks and pioneers of digital freedom by turning ideas about encryption, privacy and independent collaboration into tools readers can try.

It offers text decoding, supported token-call parsing and address-format checks. Processing is local in the browser, without a wallet, API key or RPC. Valid formatting does not establish on-chain validity, funds or transaction safety.

Reading resources below can be replaced or hidden. The standalone page and the tool entry in About have their own settings. The reference documents supported inputs and configuration.
