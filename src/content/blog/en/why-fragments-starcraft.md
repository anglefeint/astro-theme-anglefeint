In my view, the original StarCraft, including its Brood War expansion, is the greatest RTS game in human history. It is also one of my favorite games, so I really wanted to bring its atmosphere into this blog.

StarCraft II certainly does some things well. But when it comes to the plot, in my view, its story feels like the absolute worst kind of garbage, cobbled together from all sorts of painfully corny movie scenes, worn-out formulas, tacky plot outlines, and boring endings you could find in any dime-store novel. Its story is not even comparable to that of the original StarCraft and its Brood War expansion.

So the style of the Fragments page is also a tribute to the original StarCraft and Brood War: true classics that defined an era.
