In my view, the original StarCraft, including its Brood War expansion, is the greatest RTS game in human history. That is why I have always wanted to bring its atmosphere into this blog.

StarCraft II certainly does some things well. But when it comes to the story, I see a tired jumble of worn-out movie clichés, formulaic plot turns and endings you could find in any dime-store novel. It falls far short of the original and Brood War.

This page is a tribute to the original StarCraft and Brood War.
